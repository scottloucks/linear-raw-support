# Privacy Policy for Linear Raw

**Last Updated: June 10, 2026**

Linear Raw ("the App") is a professional-grade RAW converter built with privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled.

## 1. No Data Collection
Linear Raw **does not collect, transmit, or store** any of your personal data, images, or metadata on any external servers. 

## 2. Personal and Sensitive User Data
We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data, including but not limited to:
*   Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
*   Financial and payment information (except via Google Play's secure billing)
*   Device location, contacts, or SMS data
*   Health, biometric, or authentication data

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
Since we do not collect any user data on our own servers, there is no user data to retain or delete. Any data generated (converted images) remains entirely on your device and can be deleted by the user at any time through the system file manager.

## 4. On-Device Processing
All image processing, including RAW to DNG conversion, occurs **entirely on your device**. No image data ever leaves your device through this application.

## 5. Permissions
The App requires the following permissions to function:
*   **Storage/Media Access**: Required to read your RAW files and save the converted DNG files to your chosen output directory.
*   **Billing**: Used only if you choose to make a voluntary donation (tip) to the developer via Google Play In-App Purchases.

## 6. Third-Party Services
*   **Google Play Services**: The App uses Google Play Services for in-app billing. Google may collect data as governed by their own privacy policy.
*   **Firebase**: We may use Firebase for crash reporting and app stability analytics. This data is anonymized and used strictly to improve the App's performance.
